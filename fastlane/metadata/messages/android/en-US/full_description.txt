TrekUI Messages is a complete text messaging app drawn in the TrekUI interfaces. Conversations, threads and the compose bar are all drawn live on screen as a starship console or a warm Pipe Lounge, and every function works the same in each one.

MESSAGING
• Default SMS and MMS app: conversations newest first, threads with sent, delivered and not-sent state
• Picture messages through the system photo picker, and group messages to several people
• Search across names, numbers and message text
• Drafts are kept per conversation
• Dual SIM: pick the SIM per conversation
• Blocking: the system block list plus your own number-prefix rules
• Per-conversation notifications with inline REPLY and MARK READ, and quick replies from the incoming-call screen

ONE SUITE
Follows the TrekUI Launcher's interface and colour theme when it is installed, and offers its own interface picker when it is not.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your messages stay in your phone's own message store and go only to the people you send them to. Nothing is sent to the developer.

FREE TO TRY, $0.99 TO KEEP
Every feature works for free in 24-hour sessions. A one-time $0.99 in-app purchase removes the limit for good, and one purchase unlocks every TrekUI app. No ads, no subscription.

TrekUI Messages is an independent, original design. It ships no third-party recordings or artwork.
