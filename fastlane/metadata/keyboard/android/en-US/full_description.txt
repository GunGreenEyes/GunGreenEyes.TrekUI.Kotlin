TrekUI Keyboard is an on-screen keyboard drawn in every TrekUI interface. The keys look like the launcher's own controls, in whichever starship console you use, in the colour theme you chose.

TYPING
• English QWERTY, German QWERTZ, French AZERTY and Spanish layouts, switched with the globe key or a long press on the space bar
• Two symbol pages, plus number and dial pads for number and phone fields
• Shift and caps lock, long-press accents and digits
• Slide along the space bar to move the cursor; swipe left from delete to remove whole words
• The enter key shows GO, SEARCH, SEND, NEXT or DONE for the field you are in
• Suggestions from your personal dictionary and the words you type
• Key clicks, vibration, adjustable key heights, one-handed mode and a number row

ONE SUITE
Follows the TrekUI Launcher's interface and colour theme when it is installed, and offers its own interface picker when it is not.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The keyboard has no internet permission: nothing you type ever leaves your phone. It never learns words in password or incognito fields, and the words it learns stay on the device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

FREE TO TRY, $0.99 TO KEEP
Every feature works for free in 24-hour sessions. Typing never stops when a session ends; a small UNLOCK key appears instead. A one-time $0.99 in-app purchase removes the limit for good, and one purchase unlocks every TrekUI app. No ads, no subscription.

TrekUI Keyboard is an independent, original design. It ships no third-party recordings or artwork.
